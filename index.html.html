Core Themes

Research Agenda

Digital Banking & FinTech Adoption
Behavioural and structural factors influencing adoption of digital banking and FinTech services using integrated C-TAM-TPB frameworks, with focus on Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, and GCC markets.
Knowledge Management & Innovation
Examining how knowledge creation, sharing, and management practices influence innovation performance and organisational effectiveness in higher education and service industries.
Digital Transformation & Service Innovation
Cloud computing adoption in SMEs, digital platform decision-making, and the role of digital transformation in enhancing customer experience and organisational agility.
AI, Sustainability & the UN SDGs
Interdisciplinary work on the role of AI in advancing SDGs across healthcare, environmental management, and financial inclusion — engaging technical capabilities alongside social and ethical implications.
